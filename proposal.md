## Software: [Catalyst.jl](https://github.com/SciML/Catalyst.jl)

Catalyst is a Julia modeling package for analyzing reaction networks where some input objects turn into some other output objects at certain rates. It's primary stated application is to chemical reactions, but the same ideas cover a wide variety of applications such modeling ecological predator-prey dynamics, disease transmission, information propogation, and more. Catalyst is primarily meant for computational scientists and researchers seeking to build mathematical models of biological or chemical systems. Catalyst is part of the [SciML organization](https://github.com/SciML).
